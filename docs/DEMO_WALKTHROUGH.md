# A three-minute product walkthrough

Use only the fictional showcase when recording or presenting publicly. Its scripts were authored for this product; no Kuku screenplay material is included.

1. **Find promos (30 seconds).** Click Try a demo. Explain that these are recorded Gemini outputs, not illustrative scores or a fresh API call. Show the promo shortlist and episode count. Click Review promo on any card to open it directly. Upload TXT/JSON/ZIP is available for new inputs.
2. **Explain a recommendation (40 seconds).** Expand View script, then Why this clip works or Scores & source details. Show exact opening and ending evidence, the five weighted criteria, the critic's reasoning and source validation. Scores estimate editorial quality; they do not predict installs.
3. **Show a failure (35 seconds).** Open More tools → Skipped clips. One synthetic challenge includes a resolved ending. Read the real Gemini rejection and its source evidence. These challenges were deliberately constructed to demonstrate review; they were not discovered recommendations. Ranking omissions are labeled separately from AI rejections.
4. **Exercise editorial control (45 seconds).** Open Review & download, enable comparison, choose a clip, then accept/reject it with a note. Use More tools → Script timeline → Legal cut boundaries to choose a valid edit. After a timestamp change, show that the old AI score no longer applies. Invalid or mid-dialogue cuts cannot be saved.
5. **Export and benchmark (30 seconds).** Use Download promos for CSV, or More downloads for JSON and kept clips. Open More tools → Benchmark to show the complete 50-episode development run's aggregate coverage and stage counts. Explain that raw Kuku results remain private and that human precision and ad conversion lift have not yet been measured.

For private evaluation, upload the authorized pack and run Gemini with an authorized key or use the existing CLI benchmark outputs. The public benchmark panel is an aggregate record of the completed run, not a live re-analysis of private files.
